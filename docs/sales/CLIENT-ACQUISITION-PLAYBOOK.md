# Client Acquisition Playbook

Websites (₹3,000 / ₹6,000 / ₹20,000) · SEO (₹25,000) · AI B2B Lead Finder (₹10,000–₹30,000/month)

Goal: **first 10 paying clients in 30 days**, then a referral and retainer engine that keeps them coming.

---

## 0. Fix these before you send a single message (1 day)

Your posters look premium. These gaps are what will cost you sales:

| Problem | Why it kills sales | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **No phone, WhatsApp, QR or name on any poster** | Someone sees it, wants it, and can't reach you. | Add your WhatsApp number plus a `wa.me` QR code to all 3 posters. Add your name or brand. |
| **Website at ₹3,000 *and* ₹20,000** | A prospect who sees both thinks ₹20k is a rip-off, or ₹3k is junk. | Show them as **one price ladder** (Section 1) with a clear reason for each step: domain, pages, custom features. Never show the ₹20k poster to a ₹3k prospect. |
| **SEO "₹25,000": per month or once?** | Confusion means no reply. | Say it plainly, e.g. "₹25,000 for a 3‑month SEO sprint" or "₹9,999/month, 3‑month minimum". |
| **₹3k/₹6k sites give `yourbusiness.vercel.app`** | Business owners want `.com`/`.in`. A free subdomain reads as "not real". | Offer a **custom domain add-on for ₹999/yr** (costs you ~₹500–800). Most will take it, so it's easy extra profit. |
| **Zero proof (no portfolio, no reviews)** | The no. 1 objection: "Have you done this before?" | Build 3 demo sites this week (Section 3) and get 2–3 first clients at a "launch price" in exchange for a Google review and a video testimonial. |
| **Lead Finder: "Works on Android Tablet"** | Strange for a B2B service. It makes it sound like an app. | Replace with "Delivered in Google Sheets: open on any phone". |

## 1. The offer ladder (one story, not three posters)

| Tier | Price | Who | Upsell hook |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Starter Website** | ₹3,000 (+₹999 domain) | Shops, salons, tutors, clinics with no website | "Want customers to find you on Google?" → SEO |
| **Growth Website** | ₹6,000 (+₹999 domain) | Businesses that change offers or products often (admin panel, blog) | Monthly care plan |
| **Business Website** | ₹20,000 | Established SMEs, exporters, real estate, schools, custom features | SEO + Lead Finder |
| **SEO Sprint** | ₹25,000 / 3 months | Anyone with a website and no Google traffic | Monthly SEO retainer |
| **Lead Finder** | ₹10,000/month | Small B2B: manufacturers, exporters, wholesalers, IT/agencies | Upgrade to AI Outreach |
| **Lead Finder + AI Outreach** | ₹20,000–30,000/month | B2B companies with a sales team | Annual contract |
| **Care Plan** (add) | ₹499–999/month | Every website client | Recurring income: hosting checks, small edits, monthly report |

**Rule:** every website client is a future SEO client. Every B2B website client is a future Lead Finder client. Recurring revenue (Care Plan, SEO, Lead Finder) is the real business. Websites are the door.

## 2. Who to target first (fastest money)

### Websites (₹3k–₹6k): fastest to close, 1 call or 1 visit
Search Google Maps in your city for businesses with **good reviews but no website** (the listing has no "Website" button):
- Dental and skin clinics, physiotherapists, diagnostic labs
- Coaching classes, tuition centres, dance and music academies
- Salons, spas, gyms, yoga studios
- Restaurants, cafés, bakeries, cloud kitchens, caterers
- Interior designers, architects, contractors, CA/lawyer offices
- Event planners, wedding photographers, decorators
- Real estate agents, travel agents, car rentals

**Signal of a hot lead:** 50+ Google reviews, 4+ stars, no website. They are busy and making money, so ₹3k is nothing to them.

### Website ₹20k + SEO ₹25k: bigger tickets, 2–3 conversations
- Businesses with an **old, slow or broken website** (not mobile friendly, no HTTPS, last updated 2018)
- Businesses running Google or Facebook ads to a bad website: they are burning money
- Multi-branch clinics, schools, hotels, builders, manufacturers

### Lead Finder (B2B, monthly): highest value
- Manufacturers and exporters (IndiaMART, TradeIndia sellers are perfect: they already pay for leads)
- IT services and software companies, digital agencies
- Wholesalers and distributors, logistics companies, packaging, chemicals, textiles
- **Your strongest pitch:** "I found *you* using this exact system." Use your own Lead Finder to find your Lead Finder clients.

## 3. The "Demo-First" play (the highest-converting tactic for websites)

Don't ask "do you want a website?" Show them **their** website, already made.

1. Pick a business from Google Maps with no website.
2. Spend 30–45 minutes building a one-page demo with their name, photos from their Google listing, services, reviews, map, and a WhatsApp button. Deploy it to `theirname.vercel.app`.
3. Send it to them (script below). People can't ignore their own business name on a live website.
4. Close: "If you like it, ₹3,000 and it's yours. I'll add your pages and connect a `.com`."

Do **5 demos a day**. Expect roughly 1 in 5–10 to buy, which means about one client a day once you're consistent.

Keep one reusable template per industry (clinic, salon, restaurant, coaching, real estate), so each demo is a 20-minute copy and edit.

## 4. Scripts (copy, paste, personalize)

### 4.1 WhatsApp: demo-first (websites)
```
Hi [Name] ji 🙏 I came across [Business Name] on Google Maps.
Your [4.6⭐ / 120 reviews] are amazing, but there's no website, so people
searching "[service] in [area]" are going to your competitors.

I made a free demo website for you, have a look:
👉 [link]

If you like it, I can make it fully yours (with your own .com) for ₹3,000.
If not, no problem at all. Just wanted to help 🙂

— [Your Name], [Brand]
```

### 4.2 WhatsApp: no demo (short)
```
Hi [Name] ji, quick question: when someone Googles "[service] in [area]",
does [Business Name] show up with a website?

I build modern mobile websites for local businesses, starting ₹3,000
(one-time, live in 3 days). Can I send you 2 examples?
```
*Asking permission ("Can I send examples?") gets far more replies than dumping a price list.*

### 4.3 Walk-in (works very well for local shops)
> "Namaste, I'm [Name]. I make websites for businesses in [area]. I saw you have great reviews on Google but no website. I've actually already made a sample for you." *(show phone)* "If someone searches for you, this is what they'd see. It's ₹3,000 one-time, live in 3 days. Should I make it final for you?"

Go between 3 and 5 pm, when shops are quiet. Never during rush hour.

### 4.4 Phone call (old or bad website → ₹20k / SEO)
> "Hi, is this [Name]? I'm [Name] from [Brand]. I'll be quick. I was looking at [website] on my phone and noticed [it takes 9 seconds to load / the menu breaks on mobile / it's not showing on Google for '[keyword]']. Most of your customers are on mobile, so you're likely losing enquiries every day. I've put together a free 1-page audit showing exactly what to fix. Can I WhatsApp it to you?"

Then send a short audit: speed score (PageSpeed Insights), mobile screenshot, 3 keywords they don't rank for, 3 fixes. **The free audit is the product demo for SEO.**

### 4.5 Cold email / LinkedIn: Lead Finder (B2B)
**Subject:** 37 [industry] buyers in [region] you're not talking to yet

```
Hi [First name],

I run an AI lead research service for [industry] companies.
As a test, I ran it on [Their Company]'s market and found
[37] potential buyers in [UAE / Gujarat / USA], each with company
details, website, and a fit score.

Here are 5 of them, free: [Google Sheet link]

If these look useful, I can deliver 200 qualified leads every month
(₹10,000/month), or 500 leads with personalized outreach emails written
for you (₹20,000+).

Worth a 15-minute call this week?

— [Your Name]
```
**Always lead with free sample leads.** It proves the product instantly and costs you minutes.

### 4.6 Instagram DM (salons, cafés, boutiques, gyms)
```
Love your work on [specific post] 🔥 Quick one: your bio link goes to
[nothing / just WhatsApp]. A proper website with your menu, prices and
booking button converts way better from Insta. I made a sample for you,
want to see it?
```

### 4.7 Follow-ups (where 80% of sales actually happen)
- **Day 2:** "Hi [Name], did you get a chance to see the demo? Happy to change colours or photos."
- **Day 5:** "Just finished a website for [similar business] in [area]: [link]. Yours could be live by [day]."
- **Day 10:** "Last message from my side 🙂 I'm keeping your demo live till [date]. After that I'll take it down. Reply YES if you want to keep it."

Never follow up more than 3 times. Never be pushy. Be helpful and brief.

## 5. Objection handling

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| "Too expensive." | "It's ₹3,000 one-time, less than ₹10 a day for a year, and one new customer pays for it. Plus there's no monthly fee." |
| "₹3,000 is too cheap, will it be good?" | "Here's the demo I already made for you. What you see is what you get. I'm keeping launch prices low to build my portfolio." |
| "I already get customers from word of mouth." | "That's great, it means people trust you. When they recommend you, the first thing the new person does is Google you. Right now they find nothing." |
| "I have Instagram / Justdial." | "Those platforms own your customers and show competitors next to you. A website is the one place that's 100% yours, and Google trusts it more." |
| "Let me think about it." | "Of course. What's the one thing you'd want to be sure about?" *(Then handle that specific thing.)* |
| "I'll do it later." | "No problem. I can hold the launch price till Friday. After that it goes to ₹4,500." *(Only say this if it's true.)* |
| "Have you done this before?" | Show 3 demos plus testimonials. Early on: "You'd be one of my first 5 clients, so you get launch pricing and my full attention." |
| SEO: "How soon will I rank?" | "Honest answer: 2–3 months for real movement. You'll get a monthly report so you see exactly what's improving. I don't promise #1. Anyone who does is lying." |
| Lead Finder: "We already use IndiaMART." | "IndiaMART sends you buyers who are also asking 10 of your competitors. We find buyers who aren't asking anyone yet, so you're first." |

## 6. Closing and getting paid

- **50% advance, 50% before going live.** For ₹3k sites: 100% upfront is fine once you have reviews.
- UPI QR on your WhatsApp profile and website. Make paying a 10-second job.
- Send a 1-page WhatsApp summary: what's included, revisions (2 rounds), timeline (3 days for Starter, 7 for Growth, 14–21 for Business), and what you need from them (logo, photos, text).
- **Lead Finder and SEO:** monthly, paid in advance, cancel anytime after month 1 (removes risk, so they say yes faster).
- Deliver **early**. Starter promised in 3 days? Deliver in 2. That turns clients into referrers.

## 7. Turn every client into 2 more

1. **Google review plus a 30-sec video testimonial** on delivery day, while they're happiest.
2. **Referral offer:** "Refer a business friend: they get ₹500 off, you get one month of Care Plan free (or ₹500 cash)."
3. **Credit link:** small "Website by [Brand]" in the footer, linking to your WhatsApp.
4. **Upsell at day 30:** send them a report: "Your site got X visitors. Want to 3× that with SEO?"
5. **WhatsApp Status daily:** post every site you launch ("Just went live: [Business], [area] 🚀"). Your contacts are your first market.

## 8. Your own marketing (inbound, runs in the background)

- **Google Business Profile** for your own agency: "Website designer in [City]". Free, and it brings local inbound leads.
- **Your own website** with portfolio, prices, WhatsApp button and the 3 posters. Practise what you sell.
- **Instagram / YouTube Shorts / Reels:** "I built a website for a local [bakery] in 30 minutes", before/after transformations, "Is your business invisible on Google?". Post 3–4 a week.
- **LinkedIn:** for Lead Finder. Post weekly: "We found 200 buyers for a textile exporter in 3 days, here's how."
- **Local WhatsApp and Facebook groups** (business, traders, BNI chapters, market associations): share value, not spam.
- **Partnerships:** CAs, printing shops, logo designers and GST consultants meet new businesses daily. Give them **₹500 per referred client**.

## 9. The 30-day plan (numbers that get you 10 clients)

**Daily minimum, non-negotiable:**

| Activity | Per day |
|---|---|
| Demo websites built and sent | 5 |
| WhatsApp / Insta cold messages | 20 |
| Walk-ins (local) | 5 |
| B2B emails/LinkedIn with free sample leads | 10 |
| Follow-ups | All due today |
| Reel / Status post | 1 |

**The math:** about 40 touches/day × 30 days = 1,200 touches. At a conservative 1% close rate that's **12 clients**. Demo-first and walk-ins close far higher than 1%.

**Week by week:**
- **Week 1:** Fix posters (Section 0). Build 5 industry templates. Build 3 portfolio demos. Set up your own Google Business Profile, a UPI QR, and a lead tracker. Start sending demos on day 3.
- **Week 2:** Full daily volume. Goal: **first 2–3 website clients** at launch price, and collect reviews. Send 50 free B2B sample-lead sheets.
- **Week 3:** Pitch SEO audits to every business with a bad website you found. Upsell the Care Plan to all clients. Goal: **first Lead Finder or SEO client**.
- **Week 4:** Ask every client for referrals. Raise Starter to ₹4,000–5,000 once you have 5+ reviews. Goal: **10 clients total, ₹40k–₹80k revenue, at least 2 on a monthly retainer**.

## 10. Lead tracker (Google Sheet columns)

`Date | Business | Owner | Phone | Category | Area | Source (Maps/Insta/IndiaMART/Referral) | Google rating | Has website? | Demo link | Service pitched | Stage (New → Contacted → Replied → Demo seen → Negotiating → Won/Lost) | Next follow-up date | Deal value | Notes`

Review it every evening: who's due tomorrow, what's working, which category is converting best. **Double down on the best-converting category.**

---

## 11. Golden rules

1. **Show, don't tell.** A demo or free sample beats any pitch.
2. **Speed wins.** Reply within 5 minutes. Deliver before the deadline.
3. **Specific beats generic.** Mention their name, their reviews, their competitor, their keyword.
4. **Follow up 3 times.** Most deals close after the 2nd follow-up.
5. **Sell outcomes, not features.** "More calls from Google", not "responsive Next.js site".
6. **Recurring revenue is the real business.** Every client should end up on something monthly.
7. **Volume solves everything.** If sales are slow, double the daily touches before changing anything else.
