# Authority and backlink strategy

Rules: no purchased links, no link exchanges at scale, no private blog networks, no automated
directory blasting, no fake reviews or mentions. Every link should be one a buyer could reasonably
follow. No rankings are promised.

## Tier 1: foundations (owner, weeks 1–4)

| Opportunity | Type | Effort | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn company page and founders' profiles linking to weberact.co | Entity / profile | Low | Same description everywhere (geo-strategy.md) |
| Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush listings | B2B directories | Low–medium | Free listings; reviews must come from real clients through the directory's own verification |
| Crunchbase | Entity profile | Low | |
| Partner and tool directories where WeberAct genuinely qualifies (for example n8n, HubSpot, Webflow or Shopify partner programmes) | Partner listings | Medium | Only after meeting each programme's requirements |

## Tier 2: industry relevance (months 2–3)

| Opportunity | Type | Who it reaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manufacturing and export publications (for example Thomasnet's resources, Manufacturing.net, IndustryWeek; UK: The Manufacturer; Australia: Australian Manufacturing) | Expert articles, comment | Manufacturers |
| Export promotion bodies and chambers of commerce in each market (for example local chambers, industry associations) | Member resources, events, guest talks | Exporters |
| Trade show and B2B marketplace content (supplier guides, webinars) | Guest content | Exporters, importers |
| Podcasts on manufacturing marketing, B2B growth and automation | Interviews | Owners, marketing leads |

## Tier 3: earned links from original work (from day 61)

| Asset | Why it earns links |
| --- | --- |
| Original study: website speed and mobile readiness of 50 manufacturer websites in one market (method and raw data published) | Journalists and trade media cite numbers they cannot get elsewhere |
| Free tools or templates: technical SEO audit checklist, B2B lead qualification scorecard, cold email rules by country (with sources) | Resource pages link to useful tools |
| Expert commentary: respond to journalist requests (for example Qwoted, Featured, HARO successors) on B2B websites, SEO and automation | Mentions in business media |

## Outreach process (human approval required)

1. Build a list in Google Sheets: site, page, contact, reason the link helps their readers.
2. Draft the message; the owner approves every first message and every template.
3. Send personally (not bulk); follow up once.
4. Track: replies, placements, linked URL, anchor text, date.
5. Never pay for links; if a placement is sponsored, it must be marked as such (`rel="sponsored"`).

## Measuring

Referring domains and new links in Search Console (Links report) and Bing Webmaster Tools; referral
traffic and `generate_lead` events from referral sources in GA4.
