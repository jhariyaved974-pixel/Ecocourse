# Competitor analysis

Research date: 9 November 2026. Evidence: one live Google results check (OpenSEO, US desktop) and web
searches per market. **Limits**: no backlink, traffic or authority metrics were collected (Semrush had
no API units; OpenSEO credits ran out). Positioning statements below summarise what each company says
about itself on its own site or directory listing; they are not verified. Nothing here should be
copied: the aim is to find gaps.

agency service pages. Forums and galleries also rank, so the results are beatable. Weberact's
opportunity: a service page (done) plus an examples-and-checklist guide (planned), both specific to
B2B manufacturers and exporters.

## United Kingdom (web search, positioning only)

Specialist B2B and manufacturing web agencies surface: BFI (manufacturing, engineering, industrial),
Bigfork (manufacturers across automotive, engineering, machinery), Blend (UK and US; one comparison
lists projects from £30k), SplitPixel (UK manufacturers since 2008), Axon Garside (complex B2B
purchasing), Design Workshop (manufacturing marketing). Many "best agencies" lists are published by
agencies ranking themselves first.

## United Arab Emirates (web search, positioning only)

Lead-generation and SEO agencies present (Cactix, M3.agency, Bird Marketing, OwlClaw manufacturing
pages, Carril Agency), plus directory lists (Clutch Dubai SEO firms, Krowdbase). Search results
suggest few firms combine B2B websites, SEO, automation and export-focused lead generation in one
offer; most specialise in one.

## Canada (web search, positioning only)

Industrial-focused SEO firms (enOptimize, Konstruct Digital, Inspec Marketing) and many generalists in
directory categories (Clutch lists a large Canadian manufacturing SEO category).

## Australia (web search, positioning only)

Manufacturing web firms are mostly regional (Boylen in Adelaide, Visual Marketing, Titan Blue on the
Gold Coast) and focus on web design; SEO and automation are often separate.

## Comparison and gaps WeberAct can realistically use

| Dimension | Typical competitor | WeberAct now | Gap to use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Service positioning | Website **or** SEO **or** outbound | All four (website, SEO, automation, lead generation) as one system | Position as one accountable team for "enquiries end to end" for manufacturers and exporters |
| Pricing transparency | Rare; some publish minimums | Fixed fee in writing; factors published | Publish a cost guide (planned) |
| International / multi-market | Mostly single-country | Five markets, with country rules explained | International page + compliance content |
| Proof | Case studies, client logos, awards | **None yet (placeholders)** | The biggest gap against competitors; add permissioned case studies first |
| Content depth | Examples articles, guides | 5 structured service pages | Guides for manufacturers and exporters (content plan) |
| Technical performance | Not measured here | Lighthouse 100 on every tested page | Use as proof in proposals; offer a free speed check |
| Answer-engine readiness | Varies | Direct answers, FAQs, full structured data | Keep facts consistent; add authors |
