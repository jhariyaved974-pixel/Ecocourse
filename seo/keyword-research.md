estimates, not exact counts. Keyword difficulty is DataForSEO's 0–100 scale: lower is easier.

Three submitted keywords returned no data at all and are treated as having no measurable US volume:
"website design for exporters", "seo for exporters", "b2b lead generation for exporters".

## What the US data says

1. **Commercial service searches have low published difficulty.** Most service terms score 0–33. That
   does not mean easy rankings for a new domain with no links, but it does mean competition is not
   dominated by huge publishers: specialist agencies with clear service pages compete.
2. **The highest-value gaps are in Weberact's exact positioning.** "b2b seo agency" (1,000/mo, KD 1,
   CPC $128.62), "website design for manufacturers" (390, KD 2), "industrial seo" (320, KD 0),
   "lead generation for manufacturers" (110, KD 6, CPC $147.55). High CPCs show advertisers find these
   buyers valuable; low KD shows the organic results are beatable.
3. **"website redesign services" is the largest commercial term** (5,400/mo, KD 10). It belongs on the
   website development page (redesigns are covered in its copy and FAQ) and deserves its own guide later.
4. **AI automation searches are large but mixed-intent.** "ai automation agency" (4,400) is classed as
   navigational (people looking for a specific agency or for the trend), "ai agents for business"
   (3,600) is commercial. "what is ai automation" (1,300, informational) is answered directly on the
   automation page.
5. **Export-specific searches are tiny in the US** ("export lead generation" 10/mo, "how to find
   international buyers" 10/mo). Exporters search for the outcome ("b2b lead generation", "lead
   generation for manufacturers") rather than "export". Content for exporters should target those
   outcome terms and mention exporting in the copy, not in the head term.

## How to refresh this research

1. Top up OpenSEO credits (or Semrush API units).
2. Run one `get_keyword_metrics` call per market with the list above (about 22 credits each).
3. Append to `seo/data/` as `keywords-<country>-<yyyy-mm>.csv` with the same columns.
4. After Search Console is connected (about 4–8 weeks of data), add the real queries from its
   Performance report, which are more reliable than any tool estimate for an established page.
