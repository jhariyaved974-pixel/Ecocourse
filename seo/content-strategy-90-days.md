# 90-day content plan

Principle: fewer, better pages. Every piece must answer a real buyer question, link to the matching
service page, and contain something a competitor's page does not (WeberAct's own process, a checklist,
a worked example, or original data). Every piece is reviewed and approved by the owner before it is
published.

Volumes are US, verified (keyword-research.md). Other markets: measure first.

## Brief template (fill one per piece)

- **Working title** and **primary keyword** (from keyword-url-map.md; confirm no existing page targets it).
- **Search intent** and what currently ranks (check the live results; note AI Overview presence).
- **Reader**: role, company type, country.
- **Direct answer** (2–4 sentences) to open the piece.
- **Outline**: H2s phrased as questions buyers ask.
- **WeberAct-specific value**: process, example, data or checklist only WeberAct can give.
- **Sources**: official or primary sources for every fact.
- **Internal links**: to the hub service page (required) and one related guide.
- **Call to action**: the matching proposal link (`proposal.html#websites`, `#search`, `#automations`, `#leadgen`).
- **Author and review**: named author, owner approval date.
