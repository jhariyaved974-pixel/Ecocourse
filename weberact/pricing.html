Questions about pricing

Are these prices fixed?

These are the ranges we work in. After a consultation you get one fixed fee for an agreed scope, in writing. That fee does not change unless you ask for something new.

Do you price in rupees?

Yes. Businesses in India are quoted in INR, plus GST where it applies. International clients are quoted in USD.

How do payments work?

Starter projects: 50% to begin and 50% at launch. Business and store projects: 40% to begin, 30% when you approve the design and 30% at launch. Web systems are billed in monthly milestones. Monthly plans are billed in advance.

What is not included?

Third-party costs such as domain, hosting, email and software are paid to those companies, ideally from your own accounts. Ad spend is always separate.

Can we start small?

Yes. Many projects start as a Starter site or one automation and grow in phases once they are paying for themselves.
