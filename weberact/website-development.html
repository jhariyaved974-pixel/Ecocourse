What is B2B website development?

B2B website development is the planning, writing, design and build of a website for a company that sells to other businesses. It is shaped around how buyers check a supplier: they compare capabilities, look for proof of credibility and then send an enquiry or request a quote. WeberAct delivers it with a fixed scope, a fixed fee and a launch date agreed in writing.

Who it is for

Manufacturers

Capability and product pages, the specifications buyers ask for, and a request-for-quote form that reaches the right person.

Exporters and importers

Clear product ranges, the markets you supply and enquiry routes that work for buyers in other countries and time zones.

B2B and professional services

Service pages that explain what you do, who it is for and how to start, written for the people who sign off the decision.

Growing international businesses

A website that looks as established as the business behind it, in clear English for buyers in the US, UK, UAE, Canada and Australia.

How much does B2B website development cost?

The cost depends on the scope, not on a rate card. Every project has a fixed fee in a written proposal, in US dollars or your local currency, agreed before any work starts. The fee only changes if you ask for more, and any change is confirmed in writing first.

These are the things that change the scope most:

  • The number of pages and page templates
  • Custom features such as quote builders, catalogues or client portals
  • Integrations with your CRM, booking or other business tools
  • Whether we write the copy, and how much content needs moving from an old site
  • A redesign of an existing website, including redirects so search traffic is kept

Questions about website development

How long does it take to build a B2B website?

Most projects take between two and eight weeks from the signed proposal, depending on the number of pages and any custom features. The launch date is written into the proposal before work starts.

Can you redesign our website without losing search traffic?

Yes. A redesign starts with a list of your current pages and the searches they already bring in. Every address that changes gets a permanent redirect, content that ranks is kept or improved, and we check Search Console for errors after launch.

Will we be able to edit the website ourselves?

Yes. You get an admin to edit pages and content, plus a walkthrough at handover. You can also ask us to make changes.

Who owns the website?

You do. The code, domain, hosting and accounts are in your name, with documentation at handover.

What makes a manufacturing website generate enquiries?

Buyers need to find the capability they are looking for quickly, trust that you can deliver it, and know how to ask for a quote. That means clear capability and product pages, proof such as certifications and project examples where you have them, fast pages on mobile, and a short quote form on every relevant page.
