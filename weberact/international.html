Does WeberAct work with businesses in the US, UK, UAE, Canada and Australia?

Yes. WeberAct works remotely with B2B companies in these countries. Proposals, calls, progress updates and reports all happen online, in English, with fees in US dollars or your local currency. WeberAct has no offices in these countries, so the same team runs your project wherever you are.

What we plan for in each market

United States

US English spelling, and imperial units where your buyers use them. Business email follows the CAN-SPAM Act, and some states, such as California, have their own privacy laws.

United Kingdom

UK English spelling. Personal data falls under UK GDPR, email marketing under PECR, and analytics or marketing cookies need consent before they are set.

United Arab Emirates

Clear English pages for international and local buyers. Personal data falls under the UAE’s federal data protection law, and free zones such as DIFC and ADGM have their own data protection rules.

Canada

Canadian English spelling. Commercial email falls under CASL, which needs consent, and personal information under PIPEDA.

Australia

Australian English spelling. Commercial email falls under the Spam Act 2003, which needs consent, and personal information under the Privacy Act 1988.

Should we have a separate website for each country?

Only when it genuinely helps buyers. Separate country pages or sites make sense when products, prices, regulations or language differ between markets. When the offer is the same everywhere, one clear English website, with a page explaining how you serve international buyers, usually serves buyers and search engines better than near-duplicate country pages.

Country-specific pages are worth building when they have:

  • Different products, prices or delivery terms for that country
  • Different regulations, certifications or standards buyers must see
  • A different language, or local spelling and units that matter to buyers
  • Real local proof, such as projects or clients in that country
