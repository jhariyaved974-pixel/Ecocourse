What is AI business automation?

AI business automation uses software workflows, with AI models where they genuinely help, to carry out repetitive work without someone doing it by hand: replying to enquiries, qualifying leads, updating a CRM, preparing invoices or sending reports. Each workflow follows rules you approve, and anything unusual is passed to a person.

Who it is for

Sales teams with many enquiries

Every enquiry answered and followed up, even outside working hours, with hot leads sent straight to the right person.

Manufacturers and exporters

Quote requests collected in a consistent format and followed up across time zones without losing track.

Operations teams

Orders, invoices and stock kept in sync between the tools you already use.

Owners and managers

Weekly reports that write themselves, from the systems that hold the numbers.

How much does AI automation cost?

Software subscriptions and AI or messaging usage fees are paid by you, in accounts you own. What changes the scope most:

Questions about automation

What business processes can be automated?

Most repeated, rule-based work: replying to and qualifying enquiries, follow-ups, CRM updates, quote requests, invoices, order and stock updates, and reports. Work that needs judgement stays with your team, and the automation hands it to them.

How can companies automate their lead generation?

By connecting the channels where leads arrive to a workflow that replies instantly, asks qualifying questions, logs each lead in the CRM, assigns it to a person and follows up until the buyer responds. Outreach to new prospects can be automated too, within the email rules of each country.

Will automation replace our sales team?

No. It removes copying, pasting and waiting, so your team spends its time on conversations that need a person.

Is customer data safe?

Workflows run in accounts you own, access is limited to what each step needs, and personal data is only sent to an AI tool when that is necessary and agreed with you.

How long does an automation project take?

Most workflows take two to four weeks. WhatsApp automation also needs Meta’s approval, and larger AI assistant projects can take five to eight weeks. The timeline is written into the proposal.
