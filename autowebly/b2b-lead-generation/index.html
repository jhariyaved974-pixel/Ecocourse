Is there a setup fee?

Yes. Every plan has a setup fee on top of the monthly price: ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 for B2B Pipeline Accelerator, ₹1,00,000 to ₹1,50,000 for B2B Revenue Pipeline, and from ₹2,00,000 for Enterprise Solutions, depending on scope.

Which plan is right for my business?

B2B Pipeline Accelerator suits a business focused on one target industry or market. B2B Revenue Pipeline is for growing businesses selling into several segments or markets. Enterprise Solutions is for established businesses with multiple business units or international markets. The free consultation is the place to decide.

Can it work with our CRM and sales team?

Every plan includes CRM pipeline management. B2B Revenue Pipeline adds deeper CRM and sales-team integrations, and Enterprise Solutions adds custom integrations and reporting.
